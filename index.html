中文

需要什么

  • iPhone，iOS 26 或更新版本
  • 首次使用某种语言时需要联网下载一次模型，之后完全离线

怎么用

  1. 选好语言。上面两个菜单分别是「视频在说什么」和「字幕显示成什么」。
  2. 开始共享声音。iOS 会弹出一个系统面板，在里面点「开始共享」。这是 iOS 上唯一能拿到其他 App 声音的方式。
  3. 打开字幕悬浮窗，然后切到播放视频的浏览器或 App。字幕会一直更新在悬浮窗中，可以拖动或缩放。

顺序不能反：先共享声音，再开悬浮窗——悬浮窗要等声音送进来才有内容，所以 App 里也是共享开始之后才会出现第二个按钮。

免费次数和购买

  • 新用户有 3 次免费字幕，用完后每周会再送 1 次。
  • 字幕真正出现才算一次——手滑点开悬浮窗又关掉不扣次。
  • 一次会话中途绝不会被打断，看多久都算同一次。
  • 想不再计次，可以在 App 里选按月订阅、按年订阅，或一次性终身解锁。

换了手机 / 重装了 App

打开 App 底部的解锁页面，点「恢复购买」，用当初购买时的同一个 Apple 账户登录即可。

怎么取消订阅

订阅由 Apple 管理，不在本 App 内取消：打开「设置」→ 点最上方你的名字 → 订阅，选择「实时译字幕君」后取消。终身解锁是一次性买断，不会续订，无需取消。

没有字幕出来？

  • 先看 App 主界面中间那张卡片——它会写明当前卡在哪一步（模型还在下载、语言包还没装、声音没在共享）。
  • 确认视频没有静音，且音量不是最小。
  • 确认状态栏有红色录制指示，代表声音确实在共享。
  • 换过「视频语言」之后必须重新开始共享声音才生效；字幕语言可以随时换。
  • 某些语言组合这台设备可能不支持，App 会直接提示你换一种组合。

English

Requirements

  • iPhone running iOS 26 or later
  • A network connection the first time you use a language, to download its model once. Fully offline afterwards.

How to use it

  1. Pick your languages. The two menus are the language being spoken and the language you want subtitles in.
  2. Start Sharing Audio. iOS shows its own Screen Sharing sheet — tap Start Sharing there. It is the only way an app can receive audio from other apps on iOS.
  3. Open Subtitle Window, then switch to the browser or app playing your video. The subtitles keep updating in the floating window, which you can drag and resize.

The order matters: share the audio first, then open the subtitle window — the window has nothing to show until audio is reaching it, which is why the app only offers the second button once sharing is running.

Free sessions and purchases

  • A new install gets 3 free captioning sessions, and one more every week after those run out.
  • A session only counts once captions actually appear — opening the window by mistake and closing it costs nothing.
  • A session is never cut short. Watch for as long as you like; it stays one session.
  • To remove the limit, the app offers a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime unlock.

New phone, or reinstalled the app

Open the unlock screen at the bottom of the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

Cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside this app: open Settings → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions, then select Live Caption and cancel. The lifetime unlock is a one-time purchase, never renews, and needs no cancelling.

No subtitles appearing?

  • Check the card in the middle of the app's main screen — it says exactly which stage is pending (model downloading, language pack missing, audio not being shared).
  • Make sure the video is not muted and the volume is up.
  • Look for the red recording indicator in the status bar, which means audio really is being shared.
  • Changing the spoken language requires starting audio sharing again. The subtitle language can be changed at any time.
  • Some language combinations aren't available on a given device; the app will tell you and ask you to pick another.
